# Engine research workbenches

The Company tools separate reported evidence from forward-looking assumptions:

- **Briefing:** session readiness checklist, explicit gaps and a downloadable evidence snapshot. Checklist completion is not a recommendation.
- **Forecast:** price-path sensitivity, sourced estimates and analyst-coverage audits, model-rating research, growth diagnostics and an adjustable company scorecard. These are not interchangeable with analyst consensus.
- **Earnings & Reports:** reported earnings, observed earnings-release moves, annual/quarterly statements and a profit-to-cash period bridge. Old `Earnings & forecasts` URLs resolve to this renamed tool.
- **Valuation:** existing model evidence plus EPS × assumed P/E sensitivity. Missing, nonpositive or currency-mismatched EPS withholds illustrations.
- **Ownership:** same-disclosure-date filtering, holder categories, shares, percentage coverage and overlap warnings. No purchases/sales are inferred from snapshots.
- **Evidence:** a filterable dated ledger. Snapshot generation time is not treated as source freshness.
- **Technicals:** existing indicators plus historical volatility/drawdown and risk-evidence controls. Price adjustment and history coverage limits remain explicit.
- **Scenario lab:** separate growth paths, margin sensitivity and reverse-growth requirements. User assumptions carry no probabilities.
- **Peers:** fiscal-year filtering and metric sorting. A sorted table is not an investment ranking.

Workspace tools preserve independent protected reads: Portfolio never needs to request an unrelated company bundle. Portfolio adds concentration diagnostics, an evidence/risk review queue and correlation filtering. Monitoring adds check-health summaries and rule filters. Journal adds counter-evidence and next-source fields without overwriting old notes. Ask Engine adds an editable question builder, evidence-source counts and exports tied to the submitted request. It never submits automatically; existing API credentials, provider configuration and spending limits are unchanged.

Premium stock detail buttons show **Detailed Research ›** for a paid profile, but research still mounts only after a successful authenticated Engine read. Free-account triggers keep the upgrade gate; basic summaries, financial statements and return-on-capital availability are unchanged.

## Methodology references

- [CFA Institute — Financial analysis techniques](https://www.cfainstitute.org/insights/professional-learning/refresher-readings/2026/financial-analysis-techniques): period comparison, ratio and common-size context. Bridge YoY requires the exact prior year and matching fiscal quarter/currency. Margin uses same-period income/revenue; cash conversion requires positive income. Cash-profit differences do not establish manipulation or poor reporting.
- [CFA Institute — Evaluating quality of financial reports](https://www.cfainstitute.org/insights/professional-learning/refresher-readings/2026/evaluating-quality-financial-reports): evaluate cash flows and earnings with evidence and limitations, not unsupported management-quality claims.
- [Fidelity — Technical analysis](https://www.fidelity.com/learning-center/trading-investing/technical-analysis/what-is-technical-analysis): historical observations are limited, not certain predictions. Existing sourced series, valid intervals and adjustment warnings are retained.

All displayed values come from available app responses or clearly labelled user assumptions. No licensed Morningstar research, analyst targets, consensus percentages, moat claims or verified success rates are fabricated. Downloads are explicit user actions and exclude session credentials.
