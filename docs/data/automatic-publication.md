# Automatic financial and bond publication

Apply `20261011110000_document_auto_validation.sql` before the backend update. The existing financial-candidates worker now runs bounded, versioned automatic validation after bridging newly extracted tables. `backend/scripts/runAutomaticDocumentPublication.js [limit]` is the same one-off path. It records a private outcome per extraction/version so rejected layouts do not repeatedly consume resources.

Supported financial template: native PDF text with an exact full issuer name in the report header, a consolidated annual statement heading, explicit dated annual end, unique approval date, aligned year header, one KES multiplier, and exact accounting labels. Balance totals must reconcile at source rounding precision; income needs tax reconciliation and explicitly unscaled EPS; cash flow needs the three activity totals and reconciled net cash movement. Each statement is published transactionally through the existing historical confirmation workflow, with source hash, URL, page, units and validator version retained. Automatic restatements are never allowed. A conflict stops that statement and is recorded for review; any earlier independently validated statement retains its own audit trail.

Supported bond template: native-text CBK signed reopened-bond auction results, with unique aligned issue IDs, ISINs, maturities, coupon, accepted yield and face-value price. Values and maturity dates are range-checked. Auction yields never become secondary-market yields. Existing conflicting observations are not replaced. NSE secondary-market bonds and other auction layouts remain in review until their own validated parser is supplied.

OCR, mixed bank/group columns, unknown units, incomplete numeric cells, ambiguous report dates, interim/YTD periods, historical rename uncertainty and unsupported layouts retain review status. Confidence alone does not establish accuracy. No broad auto-approval of the pre-existing review backlog or publication of raw PDFs is performed. Future parser versions can re-evaluate an extraction without erasing prior validation outcomes.

The planned website domain is `continua.tools`, with an optional `app.continua.tools` app subdomain. Keep the existing stable Vercel URL and payment callback configuration until the domain is purchased and DNS configured. Resend sender-domain verification is a separate follow-up; no unowned domain is configured for live sending.

## First production validation checkpoint

On 11 October 2026 the additive migration was applied with anonymous/authenticated access revoked. The initial two bounded runs validated 125 archived extractions: three CBK report extractions passed, 122 required review, zero execution failures. The financial review queue remained at 710 pending candidates and two previously confirmed candidates; no new company financial periods were claimed. This is a validation checkpoint, not comprehensive history coverage. The worker continues on new extractions, prioritising native text and newer reports.

Published Fundamentals queries are persisted for at most 24 hours and rehydrated with their original update timestamps. Financial statements revalidate on reopen and every five minutes while mounted. Company Engine bundles have a separate user/tier-bound, one-day browser cache, cleared on logout/account switch; portfolio, monitoring and journal data are excluded. Engine backend last-good bundles refresh in the background, keeping their actual generated-at time. Account state is loaded centrally at startup and refreshed in the background; temporary connection errors are not reclassified as a Free plan. Paid backend endpoints remain independently authorized.
