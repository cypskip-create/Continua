## Approved Stock Research generation prompt

Premium realistic editorial product photograph for the STOCK RESEARCH feature of Continua, a Kenyan investment research application. Directly depict analyzing a company before making an investment decision. Landscape 3:2 hero artwork: a slim open laptop at three-quarter angle on a dark professional desk, its screen large and legible with a refined white and purple company research page headed 'Company research'. Clearly separated panels titled 'Financials', 'Valuation', 'Dividends', 'Forecasts', a historical revenue bar chart with both increases and decreases, a restrained price line chart and a few clean financial-statement table rows without monetary figures. In front of the laptop are two neatly placed printed annual-report pages with visible headings 'Annual report' and 'Cash flow', a pen and a small calculator; one subtle magnifying lens rests over a section of the printed statement as a practical research tool. No people necessary. The scene should instantly communicate financial statements, company fundamentals, valuation and testing assumptions, distinct from a personal portfolio dashboard and a social feed. Authentic premium financial technology photography, believable materials, soft studio lighting, restrained violet accents on a seamless near-black aubergine #100c18 background, centered composition with breathing room and the whole laptop visible. No abstract sculpture, pillars, engine, decorative crystal object, floating icons, buy/sell buttons, fabricated stock prices, performance guarantees, company logos or watermarks. Illustrative marketing concept, not real customer data.
